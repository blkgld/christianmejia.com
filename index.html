Hola!

I'm Chris Mejia. A front-end web developer and graphic designer with 25+ years experience. Along the way I spent some time working in film and television production, a period in my career i found rewarding. This is also where I became addicted to coffee. It was needed to fuel those long hours at the studios.

These days I live in Japan, where I’m studying the language and writing the next chapter of my life. I’ve also picked up my camera again. Photography has become my way of putting my design experience into practice, and Japan is a ridiculously good place to practice. Who knows, I might even work up the nerve to make some videos and become social media famous. 😄 Kidding. Mostly. Take a look at my photo link in the navigation to see some of my favorite photos that I have taken.

So what’s next? A small listening café. I want to create a place where you can enjoy a perfectly pulled latte, while listening to vinyl records spinning through the warm sound of a vintage Hi-Fi system. A place you come into with no rush, aplace for good music and good coffee.

The shop is a work in progress but I’ll continue to update this page as things take shape. Thanks for stopping by!

Note: This site is intentionally simple for now. It’s my playground for keeping my coding skills sharp, so please excuse the dust.
